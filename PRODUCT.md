# Time Machine
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Scan surroundings in New York City and explore a simulation of the block decades ago, with local history pinned to the panorama.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Next.js application with an existing camera, motion, review and explore flow. The landing page's single call to action opens camera capture, confirmed by the user. Image generation and scan storage depend on optional environment configuration.

## Brand Commitments
Rename Museum to Time Machine. Hero only, with the supplied tagline and bottom subtitle. Reference overlay-site's light composition and outward moving image ribbon. Opposite images show aligned past and present NYC landmarks.

## Evidence on Hand
Generated historical reconstructions are illustrative, not archival photographs.
